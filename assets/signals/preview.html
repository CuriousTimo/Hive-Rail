Hive-Rail signal faces

German light signals as they look on the track (Ril 301), each drawn once, the aspect as a class.

hv-main

H/V light main signal (Lichthauptsignal): Hp 0 (two reds), Hp 1, Hp 2; Sh 1 and Zs 1 as options

hv-distant

H/V light distant signal (Lichtvorsignal): Vr 0, Vr 1, Vr 2; the white Zusatzlicht as an option

ks-main

Ks main signal: red over green; Hp 0, Ks 1; Sh 1 and Zs 1 as options

ks-multi

Ks multi-section signal (Mehrabschnittssignal): Hp 0, Ks 1, Ks 2; Sh 1 and Zs 1 as options

ks-distant

Ks distant signal (Ks-Vorsignal): Ks 1 (green), Ks 2 (yellow)

sh-light

Light shunting signal (Lichtsperrsignal): Sh 0 (two reds), Sh 1 (two whites rising to the right)

hv-main-distant

H/V main signal with the distant signal below it on the same mast: Hp 0 (distant dark), Hp 1 or Hp 2 with Vr 0, Vr 1 or Vr 2
