Hive-Rail signal faces

German light signals as they look on the track (Ril 301), each drawn once, the aspect as a class.

hv-block

H/V block signal (Blocksignal): Hp 0 (one red), Hp 1

hv-entry

H/V entry signal (Einfahrsignal): Hp 0 (one red), Hp 1, Hp 2

hv-departure

H/V departure signal (Ausfahrsignal): Hp 0 (two reds), Hp 1, Hp 2, Hp 0 + Sh 1

hv-distant

H/V light distant signal (Lichtvorsignal): Vr 0, Vr 1, Vr 2; the white Zusatzlicht as an option

ks-main

Ks main signal: red, the green below it; Hp 0, Ks 1; Sh 1 (departure signals)

ks-multi

Ks multi-section signal (Mehrabschnittssignal): Hp 0, Ks 1, Ks 2; Sh 1 (departure signals); the upper white

ks-distant

Ks distant signal (Ks-Vorsignal): Ks 1 (green), Ks 2 (yellow); the upper white

sh-light

Light shunting signal (Lichtsperrsignal): Sh 0 (two reds), Sh 1 (two whites rising to the right)

hv-block-distant

H/V block signal with the distant signal below it on the same mast: the main signal's aspects, with Vr 0, Vr 1 or Vr 2 (dark at Hp 0)

hv-entry-distant

H/V entry signal with the distant signal below it on the same mast: the main signal's aspects, with Vr 0, Vr 1 or Vr 2 (dark at Hp 0)

hv-departure-distant

H/V departure signal with the distant signal below it on the same mast: the main signal's aspects, with Vr 0, Vr 1 or Vr 2 (dark at Hp 0)
